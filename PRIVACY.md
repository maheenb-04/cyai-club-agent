# Privacy Policy - CYAI Club Assistant

**Last updated: September 2026**

CYAI Club Assistant is an internal tool used by the Cybersecurity & AI Club at York College, CUNY, to manage club communications, including sending newsletters to club members.

## What This App Does

This app uses the Gmail API with the `gmail.send` permission scope only. This means:

- The app can **send emails** on behalf of the club's official Gmail account (cyai.club@gmail.com).
- The app **cannot read, view, search, or access any existing emails** in this Gmail account's inbox, sent folder, or any other mailbox contents.
- The app does not access contacts, calendar, drive files, or any other Google account data.

## What Data We Collect

The app stores:
- Club member email addresses and names (for newsletter distribution)
- Newsletter content and attachments created by club officers
- Club event and opportunity information

This data is used solely for the purpose of managing and distributing club communications and is not sold, shared, or used for any purpose outside of normal club operations.

## Data Security

Member email addresses are stored in a private database accessible only to authorized club officers via a password-protected API key. Club members can unsubscribe from communications at any time via a link included in every newsletter.

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to cyai.club@gmail.com.
